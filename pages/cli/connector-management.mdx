---
title: Connector management
description: Install, configure, test, inspect, and remove form0 connector packages.
---

<PageBadges />

Use `form0 connector <action> [name]` to manage connectors. The available actions cover installing,
configuring, testing, reloading, checking status, removing configuration, uninstalling, and listing
connectors.

Connector packages execute in the local Node.js process. Install only trusted packages, keep
credentials in `.env.local`, and consult each connector’s documentation for its storage behavior.
