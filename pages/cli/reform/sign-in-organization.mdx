---
title: Sign in and select an organization
description: Authenticate form0-cli with reform and choose the organization used by sync commands.
---

<PageBadges />

Use `form0 reform login` to authenticate, `form0 reform whoami` to inspect the current identity, and
`form0 reform orgs list` to list available organizations.

Select the active organization with `form0 reform scope use`. reform integration is optional and is
not required for local form0 projects.
