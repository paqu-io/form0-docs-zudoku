This page documents all attributes available on the `form` object, including application-level metadata.

<Callout type="note" title="Application-level attributes">
  Attributes marked as **App-level** are metadata not processed directly by the engine. They can be
  used when integrating with [reform](https://reformapp.io) SaaS product or when building similar
  functionality in your own application.
</Callout>

---
